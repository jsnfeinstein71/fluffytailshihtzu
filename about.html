About FluffyTail Shih Tzu

FluffyTail Shih Tzu is a small, home-based breeder in Alabama. We have been raising Shih Tzus for around 8–9 years, and our dogs live with us in the home as family pets.

Our goal is simple: raise well-loved puppies in a real family environment and make the process clear, personal, and straightforward for the families who bring them home.

Our approach

We are not a kennel. Our dogs are part of our everyday life, and that home environment matters to us.

We focus on the kind of qualities families care about most: loving temperaments, early socialization, and direct communication throughout the process.

We primarily serve families across the Southeast, and we may work with approved homes beyond our region as well.

What matters to us

We want our puppies to begin life in a calm, cared-for setting with personal attention.

We also want families to feel comfortable asking questions and understanding what to expect instead of being pushed through a complicated process.
